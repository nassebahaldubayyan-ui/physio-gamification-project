Our Therapeutic Games

Fun exercises that feel like games - based on clinical physical therapy protocols

⭐🤚

Reaching for Stars

Reach out and catch falling stars by moving your arm. Improves arm elevation, stability, and hand coordination.
🎯 Shoulder · Elbow (Biceps/Triceps) · Wrist & Fingers
🧩🎨

Matching Game

Match objects to the correct color box by grabbing and moving them. Boosts active range of motion and strength.
🎯 Shoulder · Elbow · Wrist & Fingers (No upper trapezius overuse)
🧺🥚

Catching Falling Objects

Move a basket to catch falling objects. Targets shoulder external rotation to prevent contracture and improve joint alignment.
🎯 Shoulder External Rotation · Prevents contracture · Reduces subluxation
