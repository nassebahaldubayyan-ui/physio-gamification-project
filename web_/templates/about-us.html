About Us

We're here to make physical therapy fun and effective

PhysioPlay is an innovative digital platform that combines physical therapy with the world of interactive games. We believe that therapeutic exercises don't have to be boring or exhausting — they can be transformed into a fun and engaging experience that motivates patients to stick with their treatment programs.

Our Vision & Mission

To lead the world in integrating entertainment with physical therapy, making recovery a loved experience for everyone, and to empower therapists and patients with science-backed interactive tools to achieve the best therapeutic outcomes.
