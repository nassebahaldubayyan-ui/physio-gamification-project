About rehabbo

rehabbo is a physiotherapy gamification platform that helps patients stay motivated and consistent with their rehabilitation exercises. We combine simple game mechanics with rehab tracking to make home exercises feel easier and more engaging.

Our Mission

To make physiotherapy fun and accessible for everyone. We believe that when therapy feels like play, patients are more likely to stick with their exercises and achieve better recovery outcomes.
